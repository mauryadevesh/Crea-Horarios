2. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
================================================================================

Q1: What does the Weekly Timetable Maker do?
A1: Weekly Timetable Maker is a web application designed to plan weekly academic schedules and log daily attendance. It automatically detects and prevents timetable clashes so instructors are never double-booked, rooms are never reserved twice at the same time, and lectures never overlap.

Q2: What kinds of clashes does the conflict engine prevent?
A2: The scheduling engine checks three types of conflicts:
1. Faculty Double-Booking: The same instructor cannot be scheduled in two different classes or rooms at the same time.
2. Room Double-Booking: The same classroom or laboratory cannot be booked for multiple sessions during overlapping hours.
3. Time Collisions: Two lectures cannot overlap in time on the same day.
If a clash is found, a warning banner explains the exact conflict and prevents saving until resolved.

Q3: How do I add a subject and assign a teacher?
A3: Go to the 'Timetable' tab, find the 'Subjects & Faculty' section, enter the subject name and the teacher's name (e.g. Dr. Alan Turing), and click 'Add Subject'. The teacher is automatically assigned to every class session scheduled for that subject.

Q4: How do I schedule a class session?
A4: Click '+ Schedule New Class' (or '+ Add Class' on a specific day). Choose the day, select the subject, enter or pick a room (e.g. Room 101, Lab A), and choose a period preset or custom start and end times. The system validates the slot in real time; once conflict-free, click 'Schedule Class'.

Q5: Can I use custom class hours instead of standard 1-hour periods?
A5: Yes. In addition to 1-hour period presets, you can enter custom start and end times down to the minute using the time pickers.

Q6: How do I view my schedule by specific days?
A6: In the 'Schedule Master Matrix' section at the bottom of the Timetable tab, use the 'Filter Day' toolbar to toggle between 'All Week' or any individual day (Mon through Sun).

Q7: How does the Daily Log work?
A7: Switch to the 'Log' tab to record daily lecture attendance. For each class scheduled on the selected date, tap 'Attended', 'Missed', or 'Cancelled'. You can also add notes for assignments, proxy teachers, or announcements.

Q8: What if a class is swapped or cancelled for just one day?
A8: Under 'Today’s Schedule' in the Log tab, simply click any subject pill to toggle it on or off for that date. This modifies only the selected day's log without changing your recurring weekly timetable. Click 'Reset to default schedule' anytime to revert back.

Q9: Is my timetable data saved automatically?
A9: Yes. Everything is saved automatically to your browser's local JSON database. You can also export your complete database as a 'timetable_database.json' file using the 'Export JSON' button in the header, or import an existing file using 'Import JSON'.

Q10: Does the website work on mobile phones and tablets?
A10: Yes. On mobile phones, the schedule renders as touch-friendly cards and bottom-sheet dialogs. On tablets and laptops, it displays an expanded matrix table.

Q11: How do I edit or delete a scheduled class?
A11: Every class card and table row has dedicated 'Edit' and 'Delete' buttons. Clicking 'Edit' opens the scheduling modal with pre-filled details, while 'Delete' removes the slot immediately.

Q12: How do I reset my schedule if I want to start over?
A12: In the 'Subjects & Faculty' header, click 'Reset to defaults' to restore sample subjects and slots, or click 'Clear logs' to wipe logged attendance and daily notes while keeping your timetable structure.

2. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
================================================================================

Q1: What does the Weekly Timetable Maker do?
A1: Weekly Timetable Maker is a web application designed to plan weekly academic schedules. It automatically detects and prevents timetable clashes so instructors are never double-booked, rooms are never reserved twice at the same time, and lectures never overlap.

Q2: What kinds of clashes does the conflict engine prevent?
A2: The scheduling engine checks three types of conflicts:
1. Faculty Double-Booking: The same instructor cannot be scheduled in two different classes or rooms at the same time.
2. Room Double-Booking: The same classroom or laboratory cannot be booked for multiple sessions during overlapping hours.
3. Time Collisions: Two lectures cannot overlap in time on the same day.
If a clash is found, a warning banner explains the exact conflict and prevents saving until resolved.

Q3: How do I add a subject and assign a teacher?
A3: Under 'Subjects & Faculty', enter the subject name and the teacher's name (e.g. Dr. Alan Turing), and click 'Add Subject'. The teacher is automatically assigned to every class session scheduled for that subject.

Q4: How do I schedule a class session?
A4: Click '+ Schedule New Class' (or '+ Add Class' on a specific day). Choose the day, select the subject, enter or pick a room (e.g. Room 101, Lab A), and choose a period preset or custom start and end times. The system validates the slot in real time; once conflict-free, click 'Schedule Class'.

Q5: Can I use custom class hours instead of standard 1-hour periods?
A5: Yes. In addition to 1-hour period presets, you can enter custom start and end times down to the minute using the time pickers.

Q6: How do I view my schedule by specific days?
A6: In the 'Schedule Master Matrix' section at the bottom, use the 'Filter Day' toolbar to toggle between 'All Week' or any individual day (Mon through Sun).

Q7: Is my timetable data saved automatically?
A7: Yes. Everything is saved automatically and persistently in your browser's local JSON database, ensuring your timetable is always ready offline.

Q8: Does the website work on mobile phones and tablets?
A8: Yes. On mobile phones, the schedule renders as touch-friendly cards and bottom-sheet dialogs. On tablets and laptops, it displays an expanded matrix table.

Q9: How do I edit or delete a scheduled class?
A9: Every class card and table row has dedicated 'Edit' and 'Delete' buttons. Clicking 'Edit' opens the scheduling modal with pre-filled details, while 'Delete' removes the slot immediately.

Q10: How do I reset my schedule if I want to start over?
A10: In the 'Subjects & Faculty' header, click 'Reset to defaults' to restore sample subjects and slots, or click 'Clear schedule' to wipe all scheduled sessions while keeping your subject list.
